What changes between architectures?

DCG: same exposure; switching electrical conversion gain changes input-referred noise and voltage headroom. Dual exposure: separate integrations; increased highlight range, but timing differences can create motion artifacts. Triple exposure: can lower individual transition gaps at the cost of additional capture and buffering complexity.
