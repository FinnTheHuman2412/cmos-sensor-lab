y(t) = x(t) − x(t − T_CDS) H(f) = 1 − exp(−j·2πf·T_CDS) |H(f)| = 2|sin(πf·T_CDS)|

The high-pass-like response suppresses DC and low-frequency offsets. At other frequencies the amplitude can be as high as 2, so CDS is not a universal reduction in white noise.

Reset and signal samples

In a 4T pixel, reset sampling followed by signal sampling can cancel the common reset-level offset under the relevant correlation assumptions. Different readout sequences produce different cancellation behavior.
